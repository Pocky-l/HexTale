The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [1.8.0+1.21.1] - 2026-10-31
### Added
- The Void is a place now. A breach held by six anchors no longer throws a researcher back: walk into it and it pulls
  you through, down its throat, to the other side. Its ground is a field of huge curved plates of shell lying in
  terraces over a bottomless magenta glow - soil and flesh on top, glowing seams beneath, lips curled at the edges, the
  abyss open in the gaps between them. Colossal chitin ribs arch out of knots of flesh and from plate to plate, skin is
  stretched between them, hollow husks lie sunk into the plates and digested masses hang in the gaps. Nothing of our
  world is in it. Everything curls toward the vortices at its heart.
- The way out is always open: the breach stands on the other side as a gate, warm with our daylight, on a ledge of
  chitin. It cannot be closed or broken; it follows its breach when a Breach Heart carries it elsewhere and still leads
  to where it stood if it is gone. One step in the Void is eight in our world, so held breaches are joined by a short
  road through it. A Recall Charm used in the Void pulls its bearer out to our world.
- The Void is dangerous and does not warn twice: the abyss below kills, and worn patches of skin - a shade paler, the
  veins nearly gone - hold a moment under your weight and then tear.
- Its zones: the Threshold, the Ribcage Reach, the Swallowed Shoals, the Husk Drift and the Vortex.
- Its plates are alive with detail: veins of light running across them like rivers, thickets of thin curved spines,
  bladders of skin with crystals in their bottoms, pores through to the abyss ringed with light, outcrops of Void
  crystal, banded edges of chitin and flesh, tendrils hanging from their undersides with glowing tips, bladders
  drifting in the gaps. The great ribs glow at their joints. Voidlings and Void Mites come out of its dark.
- New blocks: Husk Chitin and its lit ridge, Void Membrane (plain, veined, worn), Gnawed Stone, Riftmetal Ore and
  Hollow Crystal clusters.
- Raw Riftmetal, broken out of veins in the Void's chitin (it wants a radiant pickaxe), and Hollow Crystals -
  hextech the Void drank empty.
- Each zone has something of its own to find: membrane lakes sagging between rims of chitin in the Ribcage Reach, their
  veins running to a crystal knot; islands of swallowed stone at the level one walks on in the Shoals; husks thirty
  blocks long in the Husk Drift, lit from inside; and over the Vortex its sealed heart, a bud of chitin petals with
  light in its seams.
- The Void's own sky: a funnel of veined membrane turning slowly overhead, its veils spiralling in toward a beating
  violet eye; far ribs, curled horns and crystal-tipped spires stand in drifting mist at the horizon; below, the abyss
  glows round its own slower funnel, and motes of its air rise slowly from the dark, lilac or amber by the zone.
- Every gate in the Void stands under a column of warm light, seen from far off: the way home.
- The Void's flora: cilia carpets and tall cilia, hanging tendrils (one can climb them), spore puffs (brush one for slow
  falling, crush a picked one for longer), sinew curtains stretched across narrow gaps (they hold you back, then
  snap), spring blisters that throw you high. Pools of Void Ichor glow in the plates' hollows: thick, slow, one wades
  out of it.
- Everything found in the Void has a use: chitin and gnawed stone building sets (stairs, slabs, walls), dyes from cilia,
  string from sinews, craftable spring blisters and lit ridges, a bucket of ichor burns as long as lava, Hollow Crystals
  hold four times as long in anchors and purifiers, worn skin can be
  taken with Silk Touch for traps. The Void's matter carries its own essences (rift first of all).
- Sinew Willows grow on the Void's plates: trunks braided of two or three sinews, roots gripping the plate, boughs that
  arch out and droop, lilac fronds with glowing buds and long curtains of fronds hanging from them. Groves stand at the
  Threshold, fewer and younger trees deeper in, none round the vortex. Their wood is a full set (logs, stripped,
  planks, stairs, slab, fence, gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate) that never burns and works wherever wood
  does - a crafting table and tools can be made in the Void at last. Fronds drop sprouts that grow on the Void's soil.
- Ichor Pods hang under the willows' fronds and glow brighter as they ripen. Pick a ripe one by hand; eaten, it lets
  you see in the dark - and a few blocks into ichor. Pods and tendrils light a Husk Chitin ridge as glowstone does,
  and a pod gives magenta dye.
- Gnawed Iron Ore and Gnawed Gold Ore: the metal our world lost to the Void, in clots through the gnawed stone of the
  Shoals.
- Void Glass and its pane: a Hollow Crystal and membrane fused into glass, lilac with the skin's net in it.
- The Void Lens, a spyglass with a Hollow Crystal for its lens: looking through it, the haze draws back, and riftmetal
  ore, hollow crystals and worn skin glint far off.
- `/hextale void enter` and `/hextale void exit [player]` for operators.
- A breach opened with a Void Shard is held by a ring of anchors and lets a player through like any other (it still
  does not feed on the land or send voidlings out).

- Holding a Void Anchor or a crystal near a breach shows where its ring goes: six marks on the ground round it, a
  missing side pulsing magenta with sparks rising, a held side glowing cold. Placing anchors tells you how many of the
  six sides the ring holds.
- The Void has its own creatures now. Rib Crawlers hang upside down under ribs and plates; a dry clicking overhead is
  all the warning before one drops, lands coiled and runs at you, climbing walls. Pit Maws lie flush in the ground - a
  low bulge with a ring of short spines - and tear open into a funnel when you step on them, pulling you toward their
  throat; run, or hurt one enough, and it lets go. Seers drift over the plates, eyes in cages of petals: one that stares
  at you long enough marks you with a thin violet line, and every voidling, mite and crawler around comes for you for
  half a minute. Seers never fight themselves.
- Husk crust grows thin over hollows in the plates, paler than the shell round it. It ticks under your feet; stand
  still on it and it cracks and falls in, with the patch round it.
- Currents run through the Void in long arcs from plate to plate over the gaps, pale streaks showing their way. Step
  in and one carries you across (sneak to let go). Some of them fade every now and then and drop whoever rides them -
  their streaks thin and their whoosh sinks first.
- Fog banks roll over the Void every few minutes, closing the view to a few blocks for half a minute. The fog takes
  the abyss's magenta glow when you look down.
- Nurseries: husks the size of a hall in the Husk Drift, closed but for one mouth, their walls hung with sacs that
  hatch voidlings and mites when someone comes near. On a mound in the middle the pearl sacs hold Void Pearls - but
  some of them are mimics that twitch when you come close and burst into mites when touched.
- Void Pearls, the heart of the riftforged chestplate (in place of crying obsidian). Pit Maws carry one now and then.
- Swallowed ruins on the crowns of the Shoals: corners of our houses and halls, gnawed through, a chest still standing
  in them.
- Building blocks of the Void: Polished Husk Chitin, Husk Chitin Tiles (both with stairs and slabs) and Husk Chitin
  Pillars; and the gnawed blocks of the ruins (cobblestone, planks, stone bricks, bricks).
- Passing through a breach or a gate is felt now: it draws you in along a tightening spiral, the view narrows and
  twists and the breach's throat closes round you; the fall down the throat lasts a moment however fast the other side
  is ready, and the other side spits you out.
- Own sounds for the Void's new creatures, its traps, currents, fog banks and the passage; worn skin has its own stretch
  and tear.
- Diary pages for the Undertow, thin floors, the new creatures, the Nursery, Void Pearls and the swallowed ruins.

### Changed
- Rift Ore is called Riftmetal Ore and looks like it: dark shards of the metal grown into the chitin, sparks of violet
  light running through them. It grows in veins on the open flanks of ribs, husks and plates: a few in the Ribcage
  Reach and the Shoals, many in the Husk Drift and round the vortex, none at the Threshold. Veins glow faintly and
  throw violet sparks, so they show from across a gap.
- Void Chitin comes only from the Void's creatures; Husk Chitin no longer breaks off any.
- Void Ichor looks like a glowing magenta lava, and with your head in it you see as little as in lava.
- The Void Chitin item has its earlier, darker look back.
- A closed ring of anchors no longer wears its crystals just by holding: they cloud only while the ring is still open,
  with what the breach eats and with every passage through it. A breach once held stays held.
- A breach on clean land no longer starves away while a ring of anchors is being built round it: one tied anchor is
  enough to keep it.
- A breach opened with a Void Shard counts as a breach for the land around it: sick land near it no longer tears open
  a second one. Breaches already open side by side stay open and work as before.
- The Sealed Void Crystal is sealed with Void Membrane instead of honeycomb.
- Riftmetal is no longer forged by throwing Radiant Alloy into a held breach: Raw Riftmetal from the Void is alloyed
  with Radiant Alloy in the furnace. Void Crystals no longer grow round a held breach; they grow on the geodes where the
  Void's skin sags lowest.
- Where Minecraft 1.21.1 differs: with a shader pack on, the Void keeps the pack's sky (1.21.1's Iris cannot draw the
  Void's own sky over the pack).

### Fixed
- The Void's sky no longer jumps while you walk or run.
- A world no longer asks for a backup of its "experimental settings" when it opens: HexTale's dimensions count as
  stable now.

## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-24
Hextale leaves beta.

### Added
- Hex Foundry: a 3x3x3 kiln of Fire Bricks (hollow inside, iron bars in the middle of the top) with the new Foundry
  Hatch in the middle of its front, woken with hextech dust after it is researched. Slow to wake, but once hot it melts
  all nine of its inputs at once, up to twice a furnace's speed in each - far less fuel per piece than furnaces. It burns
  furnace fuel or draws fire and magma essence from jars nearby (pipes into any brick work too); things dropped on its
  top fall in through the grate, and hoppers or pylons feed and empty it through any brick. The hatch takes four runes
  (Forge, Radiance, Eternal Flame, Bellows, Tempering, Smelting - grains of metal in molten stone - and Lens) and keeps
  them, with its contents, when the kiln is taken apart.
- Fire Bricks: fireclay (clay, sand and gravel) baked into pale kiln bricks, with stairs, slab and wall.
- Multiblocks in the research book and in JEI are a turning 3D model that builds itself layer by layer - each block
  drops onto its place, a frame of light marks the layer being laid, empty cells glint, then dust falls and a wave of
  light wakes it. The arrows under the model pick one layer to study; below it the blocks that layer (or the whole
  structure) takes, with counts. JEI has a new Multiblocks category (drag to turn, the wheel steps through layers).

### Changed
- Every multiblock now wakes to a pinch of hextech dust (one is spent; free in creative), with a glittering puff and a
  wave of light climbing the structure. The Big Essence Jar no longer takes a hextech crystal.
- Devices still being worked out are no longer shown on the research map.

## [1.0.0-beta.5] - 2026-10-23
### Added
- Field Ward: a magic stone post with a hextech eye. Within 16 blocks monsters no longer spawn by themselves and no
  farmland is trampled - a quiet base leaves the mob cap to your farms. Spawners and animals are not affected.
- Miasma Vent: a rusted chemtech grate that chokes the hostile creatures standing over it in sickly fumes. Cheap -
  it burns the unknown essence the separator leaves behind - but slow, and no player struck them, so there is no
  experience or rare loot. Researched at the Lab Bench, made at the Hex Workbench.
- Experience Condenser: a glass column in a brass frame. No experience orb appears around it - it drinks them all
  and holds the experience as a glowing liquid, up to about level 50; a Greedy Funnel nearby pours in too. Take a level
  with an empty hand (sneak for everything) or fill glass bottles with Bottles o' Enchanting; a comparator reads it.
- Rune Shackles: a nullstone block with a ring of runes on top, laid into a mob farm floor - monsters spawn on it
  like on any block. Up to four creatures standing on it hang still in its glyph - no wandering, no pathfinding, no
  pushing - so a crowded mob farm hardly costs the server anything, and they can still be struck. Feeds on shadow
  essence; four blocks from one altar ritual.
- Hex Arc Spire: a hexsteel spike wound with brass coils that strikes the hostile creatures around it with arcs of
  charge. The kills count as a player's, so they drop experience and rare loot; named creatures and bosses are spared.
  Next to a Greedy Funnel it holds back while the funnel is full. Feeds on spark essence and takes runes (Shift, Motor,
  Chain Lightning, Flash, Dam, Lens).
- Greedy Funnel: a nullstone bowl set on a chest. Whatever dies around it never reaches the ground: the drops go
  straight into the chest and the experience into its crystal, so mob farms stop piling up items and orbs. It feeds on
  decay essence (bones are full of it) and takes runes (Shift, Radiance, Spoils, Engraving, Dam, Lens).
- The Researcher's Guide is split into three parts of the game: the first steps in the workshop, the middle around the
  Hextech Altar and the end around the Hexcore. Done steps wear the metal of their part (brass, blued steel, white gold),
  and each part has its own heart on the map: the Research Table, the altar and the Hexcore with its twisting rune
  tiles. Many devices still to come were added, moved to where they belong or dropped.
- Rune Chalk: draws runes on the floor. Runes around a Hextech Altar make its rituals quicker and glow while it works.
- Nullstone (+ polished, bricks): made in the crucible from calcite. Near a working Hexcore it drinks its anger and
  fills up, its violet veins glowing brighter, then slowly lets it out again.
- Crystal Battery: a dark cube in a brass cage that stores hextech charge from crystals and feeds the Hex Workbenches
  around it; its core glows when charged and a comparator reads it. Researched at the table, made at the altar. Takes
  runes (Cache, Chain Lightning, Dam, Lens).
- Hexsteel tools and armour: a sword, pickaxe, axe, shovel, hoe and a full set of armour as strong as diamond,
  forged on a crafting table from hexsteel ingots once hexsteel is researched.
- Arcane Cloth: wool thrown into a crucible whose water holds magic and beast essence comes out as heavy, warm cloth.
- Researcher's clothes: the first clothes of the mod - a hood, a robe, trousers and boots, each a real 3D model instead
  of flat armour, and the items in the inventory are the same models. The hood is deep and pointed, with a brass badge
  and a hextech crystal over the brow. The robe has a high collar that frames the head, a short linen cape with
  shoulder drapes and a hextech clasp, a deep blue coat of arcane cloth with brass hems, a belt with a buckle, a pouch,
  a rolled scroll and a vial of essence, and coat tails that swing with every step. The trousers are thick wool with
  linen wraps, a thigh pouch and a vial; the boots are leather with a brass buckle and a turned-down cuff. A little
  sturdier than leather; the robe takes the edge off fire, lightning and blasts. Researched at the Research Table,
  sewn on the Hex Workbench.
- Each piece takes one rune at the Hextech Altar, chosen from its own four. Hood: Banishing (night vision in the
  dark), Radiance (more experience), Breath (longer breath under water), Purification (poison and weakness wash off).
  Robe: Mending (all the clothes mend themselves), Healing (a blow may start regeneration), Fireproofing (fire on the
  robe brings fire resistance), Mirror (a blow may glance back at the attacker). Trousers: Haste (faster), Windmill
  (higher jumps), Gravity (less knockback), Shift (step up whole blocks). Boots: Lift (softer landings), Pump (easier
  walking in water), Pollen (crops and saplings by your path shoot up), Quenching (hot floors burn less).
### Changed
- The Hextech Altar needs Rune Chalk, and the Hexcore ritual takes a Crystal Battery and Regenerative Slime instead of
  two brass blocks.
- The Void (a first look, no logic yet): breaches in the world through which the Void shows. A breach is made of
  square cells with a burning magenta outline and a glow around it - teeth along the edge, loose pieces hanging beside
  it, darker rectangles and black dead cells inside, smouldering seams between them. Look into one and you see a
  throat, not a flat picture: dark coils wind down it, glassy lilac ribbons glow deeper in, and at the bottom a magenta
  heart beats behind a net of black roots - from whichever side you look. Five shapes - a gate, an eye with a blinding
  heart, a narrow slit, a scattered cluster and a breach in the ground. It spreads open from its heart and shrinks back
  into it, breathes out dark smoke and draws motes of the world in. Near it the fog closes in and turns violet, and the
  edges of your sight darken in the rhythm of its heartbeat. Deep, layered sounds for the opening, the breath and the
  closing.
- Void Shard (creative only for now): right click opens the Void where you look; sneak + right click closes the one
  you look at, or picks the shape (any, gate, eye, slit, scatter, breach).

## [1.0.0-beta.4] - 2026-10-21
### Added
- Hextech Skypack: two brass tanks of air essence around a hextech gemstone, worn on the back like elytra. Jump, then
  jump again in the air: a take-off from the ground fires its jets for two seconds like a firework rocket, then it
  glides until the next take-off from the ground. Every take-off costs a little air. Refill it from Essence Jars: right
  click a jar with the pack, or sneak beside jars while wearing it. Researched at the Research Table and made at the
  Hextech Altar (gemstone, phantom membranes, hexsteel, brass pipes, leather). It shows on the back; during the
  take-off long plumes of hextech flame burst from both nozzles with a ring of light and a roar, glints running down
  them - drawn on the flyer, so they stay with you at any speed - then thin out and die. The jets cough when the air
  runs low.
- Six runes for the skypack (three at a time): Motor (faster), Bellows (a kick when the jets catch), Dam (take-offs
  that cost no air), Breath (softer walls and landings), Fireproofing (fire resistance in flight) and Lens.

### Changed
Every particle and light effect of the mod is new: hand-made, high-resolution sprites with their own motion, each
fitting what it shows.

- Essence is alive now: once in a while a drop of glowing liquid seeps out from under the lid of a filled Essence Jar
  and floats up slowly, wobbling and changing shape as it evaporates - in the colour of an essence inside. Push a jar
  and it spills a few drops. A Big Essence Jar breathes along its whole lid.
- Essence drawn from jars into machines (Research Table, Lab Bench, Whirl Reaper, Hex Sawmill) flows as a chain of those
  living drops along a soft channel of light, gathering over the jar's lid and sinking into the machine.
- New particles everywhere instead of the vanilla ones: drops that fall and splash, bubbles that wobble and burst, soft
  steam and smoke that swells as it rises, crackling hextech sparks, twinkling glints, tongues of coloured flame, and
  rings of light that rush out when a ritual ends, a block wakes, a lens is cut or a brew splashes.
- The crucible boils, bubbles and splashes in the colour of what is in it; the drain valve pours a real stream of drops
  in the colour of the essence it drains.
- Rune procs show glints and a ring of light in the rune's colour; the altar's motes, the steles' rune shards, the
  shimmer motes and the magic tree's falling leaves are redrawn.
- Whirl Reaper whirls carry puffs of air and glints up the funnel; pylon tendrils glint at their root and tip.

### Fixed
- The drain valve's drops showed a random colour: the colour did not survive the trip to the client.

## [1.0.0-beta.3] - 2026-10-21
### Changed
A warmer, cosier look for the things you see most.

- Magic tree redrawn: a dark trunk with glowing golden sap in its cracks under a crown of white leaves that sparkle

  join it anywhere (machines draw through them, a Siphon Pylon can stand on its lid), it pours out under its middle,
  machines draw from it like from any jar. Breaking any of its blocks
  takes it apart into its glass and slabs; the essence inside is lost and no jar drops. The ground under it stays lit. The guide shows the structure layer by layer.
- Brass Pipes - slim tubes with small flanges - carry essence and water and join a machine only where it has a port;
  the machine's model grows a matching joint there. Sneak-use a pipe's side with an empty hand to set it: next to
  another pipe it shuts the joint (both pipes show capped stubs) or opens it again; next to a machine or jar it cycles
  both ways -> in only (green collar) -> out only (blue collar; a jar on that side is pumped out into the pipes) -> shut.
  The goggles and the pipe's tooltip tell what the side you look at does. The Drain Valve pours whatever reaches it out into the world while
  its handwheel is open; essence it pours onto stone soaks in and turns it into magic stone.
- Researcher's Goggles: worn on the head, they show the essences inside a jar, pipe or machine you look at; looking at a
  Research Table shows its ink while coloured streams run from each jar to the orb.
- Liquid essence swirls with an animated texture, glows at its surface, darkens in the depths and bubbles. The
  alembic's flask and the separator's vessel are round glass with framed panes, like the jar.

**Rune works**
- Hextech Altar circle: the altar, Pedestals within six blocks, Rune Steles within seven and Essence Jars within eight.
  With an item on the altar, a stele lists only the runes that do something for it (the runes a pickaxe or a
  pylon takes, and those its rituals ask for) in a tidy, scrollable list: each with its name, your stars, what it does
  on that item, its number and the essence it draws at your stars (a tooltip shows all three star levels with their essence, what it cannot share an item with
  and, for a ritual, the essence the whole ritual draws). The circle stays sensible: a rune stands on one stele only,
  an item never gets more runes than its slots (the header counts them, e.g. 2/3 on a pylon; raising the stars
  of a rune it already has takes no slot) and never two incompatible runes - such runes are dimmed with the reason; an empty altar shows the whole codex.
  Sneak-use the altar with an empty hand (or give it a redstone pulse) to start a ritual: the rings turn around the
  centre item, the pedestal items are drawn in one by one, then the essences one after another, while shards of each
  stele's hieroglyph peel off and wind in a corkscrew into the work, white-hot, cooling into the rune's colour, and
  the centre becomes something new. The runes go into the work: after a ritual every stele of the circle goes dark
  and waits for a rune to be chosen again. A ritual short of essence stalls and, left too long, collapses. Ghosts of the items a ritual
  still lacks hover over the free pedestals.
- Block of Brass.
- Hextech pickaxes: the Hextech Pickaxe (iron strength), the Refined Hextech Pickaxe (diamond) and the Radiant Hextech
  Pickaxe (netherite, fireproof), each made at the altar from the one before - runes and enchantments carry over. Their
  crystal glows in the colour of the runes they carry.
- Runes on a pickaxe, like enchantments: put the pickaxe alone on the altar, choose runes on rune steles and start the
  ritual - each rune is burned in, paid only with its own essence (more for more stars and for harder runes). Up to
  four runes on any pickaxe; thirteen runes of the codex take to a pickaxe, and a rune is as strong as its stars. A
  stele with more stars raises a rune the pickaxe already has. Some runes cannot share a pickaxe, and only one of them
  may answer the right click. A grindstone takes the runes off again.
- The guide shows a turning 3D altar burning the chosen rune into a pickaxe or the smelter; pick any rune they take to
  see what it does, how strong it is at one, two and three stars, what it costs and which runes it is incompatible
  with. JEI shows the same for every rune, next to every pickaxe recipe.
- Magic stone: stone that has drunk magic - flat plates of a dusky violet stone with nothing set into it. Throw two lapis
  lazuli into a boiling crucible, then stone, cobblestone, deepslate, tuff, andesite, diorite, granite, calcite or
  smooth stone: each block sinks, takes 6 mB of the magic, and rises out as magic stone in a burst of violet light. An open
  Drain Valve over such stone soaks it too: the essence it pours soaks in where the stream lands, one block per 16 mB. Polished magic stone, magic bricks with stairs, slab and wall, and chiseled magic bricks with the
  circle's hexagon cut into them: the stone of the ritual circle.

**Item transport: hex pylons**
- Instead of a clumsy mechanical arm, the pull of hextech: four pylons made at the altar, each its own shape - a low
  brass bowl that catches, a flat plate with claws that throws, a calcite and copper column with an amethyst crystal
  that passes on, a glass bulb that pours essence.
- Gathering Pylon: set on a chest, a barrel, a furnace or any container, it reaches for the items lying around (6
  blocks, more with runes) and lowers them into what it stands on - a furnace gets ore through its top. With nothing
  under it, it sends them along its links.
- Sending Pylon: stands on a storage and sends what it gives out (a furnace's result, a chest's anything) to the places
  you link. Right click the pylon, then right click a storage: a chest or a barrel simply fills, a furnace (or a blast
  furnace, a smoker) sorts what comes - what it can cook into the input, fuel into the fuel slot; a gathering pylon on a
  furnace does the same. Up to four links, tried in order; the same clicks again unlink one, sneak + right click with an
  empty hand unlinks all. Clicking the air or a block that cannot take items stops linking and does what it always does;
  with a block in hand a right click on a pylon places the block. A broken block drops its links by itself, and sneak +
  right click with an empty hand on a linked block unties it from every pylon. The item tooltip tells how.
- Pylons belong to whoever placed them and deliver on their behalf: they feed the Alembic, the Separator, the Hex
  Furnace, the Research Table and Pedestals like a hopper would, and the Alembic boils what they bring into its own
  essences only if the pylon's owner (online) has studied it. The goggles show a pylon's owner.
- A pylon with several links takes them in turn: each move goes to the next link that takes something, so every
  linked chest, machine or jar gets its share.
- A Sending Pylon waits until its target has room for a whole batch (a furnace with 62 ore gets nothing until 8 fit),
  and what is already flying to a target counts against its room, so no extra items fly there only to come back.
- Relay Pylon: neither takes nor gives - it catches items and essence sent to it and passes them on to its own links,
  up to eight relays in a chain. Made of calcite, copper ingots, amethyst and a lightning rod.
- Siphon Pylon: set on an Essence Jar, it draws the essence up into its glass bulb (swirling in the jar's colour) and
  sends it as glowing motes arcing along its links, like the essence drawn into the altar - into other jars, a separator, anything that takes
  essence from pipes; relays pass the stream on.
- Filter, made at the Hextech Altar (a brass ingot with string, brass nuggets and an amethyst shard): right click it to set it up - nine slots (click with an item to put it in), "only these" or "all but
  these", matched by kind or exactly (enchantments, contents), and numbers: keep N of each item in the source, or fill
  a target only up to N (scroll over a slot to set N). Right click a pylon with it to set it in (the one there comes
  back), sneak + right click to take it out. On a gathering pylon it picks what to gather, on a sending pylon what to
  send, on a relay what may pass - a few relays with filters sort a storeroom. The filter's items circle the pylon's heart.
- Essence Sticker, made at the Hextech Altar (paper, slime and an amethyst shard, six at a time): right click to pick its essence from all of them. On an Essence Jar the jar takes only that essence
  (from pipes, bottles and siphons alike); sneak + right click with an empty hand peels it off. On a siphon or a relay
  it lets only that essence through, pure. Pylons never split a mixture - it always travels whole; separating essences
  stays the separator's work.
- Sorting Matrix, made at the Hextech Altar (a hextech crystal, an observer, a comparator, glass and brass): a lattice
  of eight lenses turning on its corner around a hextech gem inside a brass frame. Set it on a chest - the dump chest -
  and it reads what lies there and sends every item home, up to eight kinds at a time: to any chest, barrel or shulker
  box within 6 blocks that already holds that item (want a chest for cobblestone? put one cobblestone in it), or along
  its links - through relays too, and a relay whose Filter names an item makes the chest behind it that item's home.
  Linked furnaces get the ores and food nobody keeps, and more of the fuel they already burn. What has no home stays
  in the dump chest; a chest that carries a pylon is never filled. Linking another pylon to the matrix sends into its
  chest, so gathering and sending pylons feed it. It holds eight links; Shift widens its reach, Motor quickens it,
  Lift moves more at once. With the researcher's goggles, the storages it sorts into show a turning ring on their lids.
- Pylon runes, burned in at the altar like a smelter's: Shift (reach and link range), Motor (pace), Lift (items per
  move), Lens. A broken pylon keeps its runes.
- The magic fetches every item: a tendril of light, two strands twisting around a swaying line, reaches out of the gem,
  closes a turning sigil around the item, draws back and drags it in, swinging a little; the gem flares as it drinks the
  item in, and on to the next gem or container another tendril stretches out and pulls it along. Several items get
  several tendrils, one after another. Links show as gold threads while you sneak or hold a pylon; while linking, a
  thread runs to your crosshair. Soft whooshes for pulling and sending, a low touch for landing.
- Researcher's Goggles: every link around shows while worn; looking at a pylon shows its reach, pace, link range, its
  links (where to, how far) and runes, a gathering pylon's reach lights up as a ring on the ground; looking at a linked
  block shows which pylons send to it.
- Decorative: Magic Stone Pillar (like a quartz pillar) and Brass Grate (see-through, like a copper grate).
- Built to be cheap: items move straight between inventories in one transaction (only the fetching is drawn), idle
  pylons look four times rarer, and the clients get one small message per move.

**Farming: the Whirl Reaper**
- Whirl Reaper: a brass mast on a foot of whorled magic stone, three framed sails turning round it and a hextech
  crystal floating on top in a little whirl of its own. Researched at the Research Table and made at the altar
  (hextech crystal, feathers, an iron hoe, brass, polished magic stone; wind, plant and crystal essence). Set among
  the beds, it sends a whirl of wind to the nearest ripe plant within 4 blocks (3 up and down): the whirl leaves the
  crystal, flies over to the plant and stands on it as a turning funnel of light, crumbs of the plant swirling up it.
  - Wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot (and modded crops), nether wart, cocoa and sweet berries are reaped where
    they stand: they start again from a sprout, one seed stays in the ground, the harvest falls on their own block.
  - A melon or pumpkin on its stem is lifted off into the whirl, spun and broken open where it lay.
  - Sugar cane, cactus and bamboo three blocks tall are twisted off above the root; the pieces rise turning round
    the funnel and drop beside the root (a cactus would burn them on top of itself).
  - Every whirl drinks wind essence from a jar within 5 blocks (1 mB for four whirls; feathers boil into wind); the
    jar's stream runs into the crystal. Without wind it stands becalmed, its sails barely turning.
  - A Gathering Pylon nearby picks up the harvest. Runes at the altar: Shift (wider field), Motor (whirls more often),
    Lens; a broken reaper keeps them. Right click shows its field, pace and wind; Jade too.
  - Light on the server: it reads its field block by block only when it looks, and an idle reaper looks more and
    more rarely; the clients play each whirl from one small message.
- Whorled Magic Stone: polished magic stone with a carved spiral (stonecutter).
- Hex Sawmill: a dark oak stump with a hextech saw disc of blued steel turning in a slot under a brass guard, a
  crystal floating over it. Researched at the Research Table, made at the altar (hextech crystal, iron axe, dark oak
  logs, an oak sapling, brass; wind, wood and metal essence). It throws a glowing copy of its disc at the nearest grown
  tree within 6 blocks (3 up and down): the disc flies over, saws through the trunk in sawdust and sparks, and the whole
  tree tips over away from the mill, slowly and then faster, and breaks apart where it hits the ground. Every log and
  every leaf of it is taken; the drops (logs, saplings, sticks, apples) land on its stump and a sapling from them is
  planted where it stood - four on the four feet of a big tree when four dropped.
  - Only trees grown from the ground are felled: logs on dirt with grown leaves. Log walls, pillars and trees whose
    leaves were placed by hand are never touched; roots under the ground stay.
  - Each tree takes 2 mB of wind essence from a jar within 5 blocks (the jar's stream runs into the crystal); without
    wind it stands becalmed. Runes at the altar: Shift (farther trees), Motor (saws more often), Lens; a broken sawmill
    keeps them. Right click shows its grove, pace and wind; Jade too. Sounds: the disc whirring off, the bite into
    wood, the trunk creaking over, the crash and the rustle of leaves.

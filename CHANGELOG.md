## [1.0.0] - Unreleased

### Added
- Research Table: a two-block dark oak writing desk with brass trim, a drawer cabinet, a bookshelf, a book rest for
  the diary, an inkwell and a leather writing pad. Unstable hextech hangs over the parchment from a brass arm, turning
  inside spinning gimbal rings with motes circling it; while someone draws, glyphs stream from the parchment into the
  orb, and a freshly fused rune rises over the desk as a glowing hieroglyph. The desk glows faintly while an orb lies on it.
- Rune drawing on the Research Table: draw two simple runes (action and target) and fuse them into one of 72 complex runes.
- Rune reference: with your diary on the table, its page beside the parchment lists all nine simple runes; click one
  to see its outline in the circle and trace it.
- Unstable hextech from lightning striking a lightning rod on an amethyst block.
- Researcher's Diary with a codex, hints, notes and milestones; brass, hextech ink, rune brush and rune scrolls.

# Changelog

All notable changes to this mod are documented in this file.
The format is based on [Keep a Changelog](https://keepachangelog.com/en/1.1.0/),
and this project adheres to [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org/spec/v2.0.0.html).

## [Unreleased]
### Added
- Radiant tools are wrapped in quiet lightning from pommel to tip - short arcs snapping here and there, a few sparks
  winding round, and now and then a discharge running the whole length. The abyss seeps out of riftforged tools:
  small clouds of the Void well up along them, sink and thin out, violet tendrils creep out and twist away, and now
  and then a short tear into the abyss flares.
- Building blocks of the new metals: blocks of charged hexsteel, radiant alloy and riftmetal (nine ingots, beacon
  bases), hexsteel plating and radiant trim cut from them on a stonecutter, and void carapace tiles.
- Radiant and riftforged Bulwark armour carries the same lightning and abyss, thinner, along the head, body, arms
  and legs it covers - on the wearer, on armour stands and on the icons.
- Two blade runes for the sword, the Tendril Dagger and the Strike Gauntlet: Backstab (a blow from behind lands
  25/40/60% harder) and Bleeding (a cut may keep bleeding for 3/4/5 seconds). Their codex runes were Eclipse and
  Blight and had no use until now.
- Strike Gauntlet, made on the Hexcore: hold Use to charge it in three beats, release to punch. At a block within
  reach it breaks it - a 3 x 3 face from the second beat, two deep at the third; into the air it sends a shockwave
  that hurts and throws back the creatures in front. Held too long, it vents its steam and starts over, never hurting
  its wearer. It takes the sword's runes, three of them.
- Tendril Dagger in four levels, researched and refined with the tools of its level: a quick blade that takes the
  sword's runes. In the off hand beside another weapon it now and then finds a gap and strikes along with the blow
  (30-45% by level). Held and released after a
  short swing it is thrown, bites the first creature it meets and always comes back along a tendril of light.
- Bulwark armour in four levels (Bulwark, Refined, Radiant, Riftforged), each set opened by one research and made
  from its level's materials like the tools. Every piece takes the runes of the clothes piece of its slot - one at
  the first level, up to four. The cuirass carries a crystal heart: raw hextech clicked onto it charges it, and while
  charged it soaks 30-60% of every blow armour can stop (a Crystal Battery nearby keeps it fed too). Every level has its own look - brass over blued steel, gunmetal with lit crystal slits, white gold with violet
  wings, riftmetal with chitin horns - and the heart glows as bright as it is charged. Run dry, it cracks open in a flash of tumbling crystal shards that throws the
  creatures round you back, and soaks nothing until it is charged full again.
- Radiant Alloy: the Hex Foundry at full heat melts two gold, a Charged Hexsteel and Gemstone Dust (the bright
  cuttings every gemstone finish now leaves on the Lapidary) into two ingots of white gold; Refined Shimmer does
  instead of the dust, quicker, but it thins the land. Ore melts beside an alloy as before.
- Radiant tools are made on the Hexcore only, from two Radiant Alloy, a gemstone and a vial of Acid that etches the
  light into the metal.
- Charged Hexsteel: a hexsteel ingot laid into a Crucible that holds lightning and metal comes out charged - the
  metal of the refined tools. Coolant splashed on hexsteel lying on a lit Chem Burner charges it at once, but the
  fumes thin the land.
- Engraved Diamond: the Lapidary with a master cutting disc cuts a diamond to a brilliant and engraves it (a cracked
  diamond comes back whole). Refined tools are now made of two Charged Hexsteel and an Engraved Diamond instead of
  gemstones and diamonds.
- Refined and Radiant Axe, Shovel and Sword: refined at the altar like the pickaxe (the same research opens all four
  of a tier), with every rune carried over. Refined tools hold two runes, radiant ones three.
- Riftforged Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel and Sword: a radiant tool forged on the Hexcore with two Riftmetal, a Sealed Void
  Crystal and a raw Void Crystal holds a fourth rune and keeps every rune it had.
- Riftmetal: Radiant Alloy thrown into a held breach is kept a while and given back black, with violet sparks inside.
- Void Crystals grow on Void Rock round a held breach; set in nullstone at the workbench they become Sealed Void
  Crystals. Void Chitin softened in a crucible that holds decay becomes Void Carapace.
- Runes can be burned in weaker than you know them: click a star in the stele's list to pick the strength (the number
  and the price follow). A 3-star Drill no longer forces a 5×5 hole - pick one star for 3×3. A rune already on the item
  is set to exactly the picked strength, weaker too; without a pick a rune is only ever raised, as before.
- Five new weapon runes from runes of the codex that no tool took before (their scrolls now turn up in chests):
  - **Shockwave** (sword, axe): a charged swing at the ground sends a wave rolling ahead through it. The ground heaves
    block by block and spits crumbs of itself, a glowing crack runs along it, and the creatures in its way are hurt and
    tossed into the air. The weapon rests a moment afterwards.
  - **Blink** (sword): right click at a creature to step through the shadow to its back and strike it harder.
  - **Detonator** (sword): a creature the sword kills bursts and hurts the ones around it.
  - **Whirlpool** (sword): a blow may open a whirlpool that drags the creatures around into it.
  - **Whirlwind** (sword): every third blow in a row sweeps all around you in a circle of light.
  A weapon takes one rune worked by hand (Shockwave or Blink). Two runes of the codex got names that suit them:
  Grounding is now Shockwave, Updraft is now Whirlwind.
- Six new runes for the researcher's clothes, the Skypack, the Crystal Battery and the Arc Spire (four of them from runes
  of the codex nothing took before, so their scrolls now turn up in chests):
  - **Wavewalk** (boots): walk on water as on ground and 15/25/35% faster there; sneak to sink. Caught just under the
    surface, you are lifted back onto it. Every stride spreads rings of light over the water with a splash of
    droplets. It replaces Pump - the boots still swim as easily under the water as Pump made them.
  - **Static** (robe): a creature that strikes you in close combat gets a spark back 25/40/55% of the time - 3/4/5
    damage and a moment numbed (Slowness IV), drawn as a twitching bolt from the robe. The rune of the codex Conductor
    is now called Static.
  - **Mirage** (hood): when a blow leaves you under 40% health you fade for 3/4/5 s. The creatures hunting you lose you
    and go to look where you stood, where a shimmering figure of light stays behind; until you strike, none of them can
    take you for a target. The hood then rests 60/45/30 s.
  - **Flamethrower** (Skypack): while the jets thrust, a tongue of fire licks 3/4/5 blocks behind and below you. The
    creatures in it catch fire for 3/4/5 s and are burned a little; blocks never catch fire.
  - **Sunlight Cell** (Crystal Battery): under the open sky by day, in dry weather, the battery charges itself by
    6/10/15 a minute, drinking a soft shaft of sunlight.
  - **Beam** (Arc Spire): its strikes come as a hot beam of light that sets the creature on fire for 3/5/7 s, so the
    animals it fells drop their meat cooked.

### Changed
- In JEI, R on a HexTale item shows how it is made first: the researcher's notes moved from JEI's Information tab,
  which always came first, to a Notes tab of their own after the recipe tabs.
- The later gear asks for more at the altar: every refined, radiant and riftforged tool, Tendril Dagger and Bulwark
  piece now takes six to eight pedestal items instead of two to five, with vanilla finds that fit the tool - obsidian,
  redstone and TNT for the refined pickaxe, a brush and moss for the shovel, a lightning rod and a bottle o' enchanting
  for the radiant sword, echo shards, ender eyes, dragon's breath and shulker shells for the riftforged gear. The
  Recall Charm also wants a compass and a chorus fruit, and the Soul Flask a lead and a soul lantern.
- Each pedestal item unsettles the Hexcore a little less, so the bigger late rituals are no angrier than before.
- Recipes were gone over at every bench so that each thing reads as what it is, with less brass everywhere:
  - the Alembic is a copper still now, and the Separator takes a hopper and iron instead of looking like the Alembic;
    the Pump is half iron;
  - the Rune Stele wants a glow ink sac for its letters, and the Rune Circle a stick of Rune Chalk to draw it;
  - the Tendril Dagger is no longer the cheapest thing on the bench: two iron, two brass, a raw orb and a lead for
    its tendril;
  - the first Bulwark is built over a real iron piece (helmet, chestplate, leggings or boots) with brass plates,
    leather or chains;
  - hexsteel tools and armour need one diamond each for the edge or the plate, so they no longer replace diamond
    gear for a handful of iron and dust;
  - the Sealed Void Crystal is set in tinted glass and sealed with honeycomb besides the nullstone, and the Warding
    Lamppost costs 3 charge instead of 6;
  - each piece of the researcher's clothes has a thing of its own: a feather in the hood, a notebook in the robe, a
    compass in the trousers, rabbit hide on the boots;
  - the Impulse Plate takes a piston and the Levitation Plate a feather; the Sawmill a stonecutter, the Whirl Reaper
    a composter, the Experience Condenser a book, the Spawner Cage and the Void Anchor chains;
  - every Lab Bench experiment for a device is sealed with a sample of its own (tropical fish for the Axolotl Tank,
    gunpowder for the Chem Boiler, magma cream for the Chem Burner, sugar for the Chem Still, rotten flesh for the
    Disintegrator, redstone for the Pink Brazier, an amethyst shard for Synthetic Crystals).
- Every level of tools and Bulwark armour mends with its own metal at an anvil: brass, Charged Hexsteel, Radiant
  Alloy, Riftmetal.
- The refined research shows up once the level's materials and the Runed Pickaxe are known - no need to make the
  axe, shovel and sword first. The first Bulwark set comes beside the runed tools instead of after hexsteel.
- The chemtech shortcuts of the Arsenal cost what they save: quenched hexsteel thins the land three times as much as
  before and a violet plume rises from the burner, and Radiant Alloy from Refined Shimmer thins it three times as much
  too.
- The Separator heats over magma or lava too - slowly, with no blaze powder at all.
- The Bulwark heart takes at most 4/6/8/10 points of a single blow by level, so a crushing blow is still felt.
- The Whirlwind sweep is a softer crescent in the rune's colour, without the flat white sweep of a vanilla sword over it.
- A few finds of the Void - Void Chitin, Void Crystals and the Void's plants - now have to be studied under the loupe
  before the diary knows what to make of them.
- New ideas in the margins of the diary: where the level materials, the clouded crystal, the Void's flowers,
  the shimmer moss and the magic wood could lead. A few old ideas that came true got new directions.
- Runes now grow with the tool: a first Runed Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel or Sword holds one rune, a Refined Pickaxe two and
  a Radiant Pickaxe three, a Riftforged one four. Runes already burned into your tools stay and keep working; only new runes past the
  limit are refused.
- Chain Lightning on the sword is felt now: it fires more often (20/30/40%), its spark always bites the struck creature
  for extra damage with a visible arc, and then leaps on - to hostile creatures, or from an animal to others of its
  kind. It never touches players, pets or villagers. Before, it did nothing at all unless another hostile creature
  stood near.
- The Engraved Diamond shows its engraving: a glowing golden sign is cut into the middle of its table face, easy to
  tell from a plain diamond in the inventory.
- The purple crystals no longer look like amethyst and each other: Void Crystals (the item and the sealed one) are
  black inside with a sharp violet edge, Synthetic Crystals are shimmer pink, a Clouded Crystal is a cut hextech crystal
  gone smoky grey, and the Void Carapace is a ridged armour plate instead of a darker lump of chitin.
- Radiant gear looks like white gold now instead of bone and lilac: the Radiant tools, the Radiant Tendril Dagger, the
  Radiant Bulwark, the Radiant Alloy Ingot and its block and trim are pale gold with gold trim and walnut grips, with
  violet left only as a thin seam, so they no longer look like the Riftforged level.
- Calmer blocks that sit better in a build: brass (Block of Brass, alembic, sorting matrix and other brass parts) is
  smooth aged metal instead of a striped cartoon gold, Dormant Hextech is a softer blue with frosted glints, Chem
  Tubes are dark green glass held by iron bands instead of neon stripes, the Block of Hexsteel is a plain brushed
  plate, the steel of the World Anchor, Rift Gates and Grapple is blued, and Magic Leaves grow in white clumps with
  only a few golden glints.
- The Rune Circle stands out on a pale floor: its outer ring of stones is darker, and light-blue light glows in its
  grooves instead of a dark navy line.
- Riftforged Bulwark reads as armour now, not a violet lump: black riftmetal plates with lit rims and blackened brass
  rivets, violet carapace on the shoulders, chest, back and knees, and a sealed Void crystal heart - black core,
  violet rim, a pale nullstone band - held by the claws on the chest.
- The Runed Sword and the Refined Sword have real blades now: the Runed Sword a tapered steel blade with a bright
  edge and a glowing rune fuller, the Refined Sword a broad blade with a long point and its crystal core set in a
  dark spine, held by steel bands.
- The Chem Boiler looks like a working boiler now: an olive riveted drum of plates with a bright copper band, a round
  pressure gauge whose scale runs from green to red, and a stepped dome with a copper neck under the safety valve. It
  no longer flickers from afar.

### Fixed
- JEI shows where the Engraved Diamond comes from: a diamond cut with the Master Cutting Disc on the Lapidary.
- The Researcher's Diary no longer drags the frame rate down while it is open: its star map ran at about half the
  frame rate of the game behind it and now costs about as much as an inventory screen. The links between the steps are
  traced once instead of every frame, the hazes behind the steps are drawn as one picture each, and nothing outside the
  view is drawn.
- Opening a rune stele no longer freezes the game for a moment: the runes on screens are now painted in the
  background, several times faster, and once per size instead of once per colour, so hovering them costs nothing.
- The wisps an Eternal Flame pickaxe leaves in the dark are no longer put out by the next swing: a tool passes through
  them, only an empty hand puts one out. Each one is now a small magic flame of blue fire: a white core, tongues
  that flicker and sway, embers rising off it; under a ceiling it hangs a little higher.
- Items can be put on a Rune Circle from a few steps away again: the click used to be thrown away by the server unless
  you stood close (the same for every device that spreads past its block, such as the altar and the furnace).
- Relay and Siphon pylons no longer take runes that do nothing on them: a relay takes Reach only, a siphon the runes
  that move essence (Reach, Pulse, Levity). Runes already burned into them stay but still do nothing.
- Grapple Gauntlet: the Pulse (reel speed) and Reach runes work again - the wearer's own game never learnt them.
- Ore glow of the pickaxe's and the Tuning Fork's Insight no longer stays forever after the chunk unloads or the world
  is closed, and a held Tuning Fork keeps one glow per block instead of piling up new ones.
- Pickaxe Drill and shovel Erosion dig the face that was struck, also through a one-block wall.
- Pickaxe Eternal Flame no longer leaves wisps in daylight: the light around the broken block counts.
- Pickaxe Breath feeds clearly more often and more; Radiance gives a point or two of experience on ores that give none
  (iron, copper, gold).
- Trousers Haste is a felt speed-up now (+10/15/20%).
- Boots Pollen grows the nearest crop or sapling on the path instead of a random spot that was often empty.
- Slumber on the Levitation Plate and the Soul Flask puts creatures really to sleep: even skeletons stop shooting. They
  always wake again, also after the chunk was unloaded or the world closed.
- Rune texts: Impulse Plate Breath, Skypack Fireproofing, Grapple Gravity (now shows the yank speed), units on Null
  Lantern Banishing, Recall Charm Reach and Mending, Tuning Fork Insight, Levitation Plate Breath and Slumber, Soul
  Flask Slumber.
- Warding Lamppost Healing at high stars really heals (Regeneration was restarted before its first heal).
- Sawmill Growth makes the replanted sapling shoot up into a tree, as its text says.
- Chem Boiler Steam draws mixture from jars as fast as it cracks it; Thrift and Purification count fully at every star.
- Crystal Purifier and World Anchor Reach grow with every star.
- Greedy Funnel Engraving drops heads more often (3/5/8%) and shows when it does.
- Sword Engraving takes heads far more often (5/10/15% instead of 1-3%).
- Hood Mirage works around zombies, skeletons and other creatures that hunt by plain goals: it used to break off at
  them and leave the wearer seen.
- Disintegrator Thrift adds essence also to things worth a single point; Crystal Battery Thrift rounds its bonus up;
  Axolotl Tank Healing keeps working while the slime output is full; Crystal Purifier Thrift gives free draws also
  without light at hand.

## [1.6.1] - 2026-10-30
### Fixed
- A player who knew very much - the Scroll of Omniscience in a big modpack, or simply thousands of studied items - was
  disconnected on joining and could never get back into the world ([#4](https://github.com/Pocky-l/HexTale/issues/4)).
  Such worlds load again with this version.
- The Boiling page in [JEI](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/jei) no longer vanishes when another mod's item
  breaks JEI's item comparison (seen with [PackagedAuto](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/packagedauto)).
- A Soul Flask refuses a creature that carries too much (a chested mount full of shulker boxes, or of flasks): the
  holder of such a flask was disconnected at every join.
- A Pylon Filter keeps only the picture of what is laid into its slots: filters of filters (or of filled shulker boxes)
  could grow into an item too big to send.
- The Crucible no longer loses results when it makes more than a stack at once and the world saves in that moment.

## [1.6.0] - 2026-10-29
Hextech against chemtech: the quick, cheap and dirty road against the slow, clean one.

### Added
- Chem Boiler (worked out at the Lab Bench, made at the Awakened Workbench): a riveted pressure tank that sits on a Chem
  Burner and cracks mixtures of essence - piped in, poured from a bottle, or drawn by itself out of plain jars of
  mixture nearby. Each time the pressure is up the safety valve knocks and a batch splits into all its essences at once,
  each running into a jar nearby that wears its sticker. Quick and needing no choice, but crude: part of every batch
  goes up as fumes that wear the land thin, part burns down into murk that drips into the burner and feeds its flame.
  Hextech lamps near it flicker in the fumes. Takes runes in the Rune Circle: Steam, Thrift, Purification and Purifying
  Fire.
- Warding Lamppost (researched at the Research Table, made at the Awakened Workbench): a two-block street lamp with a
  gemstone in its head that burns light essence from a jar nearby. In its light a third of everything that wears the land
  thin - boiling, brewing, cracking, spilling - burns away before it reaches the ground, drawn into the gem as a thread
  of dark; the land around never creeps into its own chunk, and the haze clears in its light. Standing lit costs
  nothing, only burning does. Takes runes in the Rune Circle: Reach, Purification, Thrift, Healing and Insight.
- Shimmer in the veins: every vial of refined shimmer leaves a violet vein on the drinker (five at most), and while the
  body is fed the veins give - a heart, a little more damage and a little more size each, eyes that see in the dark from
  three veins, tireless hands at five. The feeding wears off after a while, sooner the more veins there are; then the
  gifts are only withheld until the next vial, nothing is taken. A bottle of light essence washes one vein out. The
  veins show on the player, glowing and pulsing while fed. Two new diary steps.
- Decorative blocks for chemtech: Chem Tubes (glass tubes of glowing green chemistry in iron bands, from chem glass,
  iron and a vial of glow mix) and the Rusted Grate (cut from rusted plating).
- The Rune Circle, a carved stone circle in the floor made at the Awakened Workbench and learned together with the altar.
  Rune steles stand round it now: lay a tool, a device or a blank scroll in the middle and the steles' runes are burned
  in, or one is copied onto the scroll, the way the altar used to do it.
- Shimmer veins show beside the other effects: "Shimmer in the Veins" with their number as its level and the time the
  feeding lasts, "Fading Veins" once it runs out.

### Changed
- Runes are written in a new script: each element is a flowing sign of a brush - a flame, waves, a crescent, a bolt - and
  a rune joins its two elements in one stroke with a swash beneath. On screens they are drawn smooth and glowing instead
  of on a pixel grid; on the steles, in the circle and in the air they write themselves the same way.
- Fewer things carry "Hex" in their name: the Awakened Workbench and Awakened Furnace, the Ritual Altar, the Lapidary,
  the Foundry, the Whirl Sawmill, the Arc Spire, the Crystal Lamp, the Warding Lamppost, the Crystal Purifier, the
  Skypack, the Tuning Fork, and the Runed Pickaxe, Axe, Shovel and Sword (Refined and Radiant Pickaxe). Hextech itself -
  its dust, crystals, gemstones - keeps the name. Nothing in existing worlds changes but the names.
- Runes with plain workshop names took older ones: Reach (was Shift), Pulse (Motor), Thrift (Dam), Levity (Lift),
  Wavewalk (Pump), Updraft (Windmill), Thirst (Sponge), Silence (Insulator), Lodestone (Magnet), Delving (Drill),
  Cutting Wind (Blades), Kindling (Bellows), Beacon (Signal), Hoard (Cache), Gilding (Photosynthesis).
- Runes that no device takes yet are out of the world: they no longer lie in chests, their scrolls cannot be read, and
  their names read "???". The codex counts only the runes there are to find.
- Looking at the Research Table in goggles no longer sends streams of motes from the jars into it; the goggles' plate
  still lists the ink.
- The Crystal Purifier and the Void Anchors also take a Synthetic Crystal: it holds less than a cut crystal and, spent,
  crumbles to a pinch of dust instead of clouding over.
- Chemtech starts to look like the other road: rusted iron, copper and a toxic green glow instead of walnut and brass.
  The Disintegrator is now a riveted iron vat with a copper rim, a pool of acid glowing inside, a porthole and a vent
  stack.
- The Axolotl Tank is a riveted iron frame round stained chem glass, with a copper feeding hatch, a little tap at the
  bottom and greener water.
- The Chem Burner wears the same riveted iron with a copper band and a copper-rimmed firebox.
- Rusted plating looks like rusted iron now (dark plates, rust patches, rivets) instead of brown dirt; the Miasma
  Vent, chem glass and the Chem Still follow it, and the still's pot is rusty copper over an iron tub.
- The Pink Brazier's cage is iron with a copper band instead of hextech steel and brass.
- The Lab Bench stands on a riveted iron frame under its tiled top.
- The Experience Condenser is a hextech vessel now: an eight-sided glass column in a brass frame on a walnut
  foot, and the experience flows into a small crystal on its lid.
- Chem vials have an iron cap, and the glow mix, acid and raw shimmer brews are a little softer in colour.
- The Ritual Altar (the Hextech Altar until now) only makes things; burning runes in and copying them moved to the Rune Circle, so making a tool
  and inscribing it happen in two different places.
- Rune steles turn to face the middle of their Rune Circle, at any angle, so a ring of them set diagonally looks in.
- Hextech grades sit where they belong: the best grade, the gemstone, goes only into the best devices (Hexcore, World
  Anchor, Skypack, the refined and radiant pickaxes). The Crystal Purifier is made with a cut crystal (the grade it
  holds), the Lamppost takes its crystal from its lamp and nullstone instead of a gemstone, and the pylons show the
  crystal they are made with.
- The Awakened Workbench and the Crystal Battery run on raw hextech only - unstable orbs and crystals grown in shimmer; cut
  crystals and gemstones are no longer burned as charge. Rift Gates are linked with a raw orb instead of a cut crystal.

### Fixed
- With shader packs that draw soft translucent light as solid (BLISS), the glow behind the essence signs over a
  crucible and other hextale lights no longer turns into a blob of light; with a pack on, the pack's own glow is used.
- With such shader packs a crucible's water and the essence signs over it are no longer blown out to white: hextale's
  liquids and signs stop counting as light sources while a pack is on.
- The ink vials on the Research Table's desk sit in their frames whatever the number of inks.

## [1.5.0] - 2026-10-28
The first part of a visual and sound update: HexTale now sounds like itself, runes show themselves when they work, and
the altar draws its circle on the ground. No more borrowed chimes, beeps and hisses at the mod's magic moments, and the
Void's creatures have voices of their own. The workshop has a new look too: machine screens of walnut and brass, new
models for the altar, the Hexcore, the jars, steles, furnace and lapidary, one family of essence signs and carved stone.

### Added
- When a rune works, a flat hexagon of light opens round the spot and the rune's own glyph flares over it, in the rune's
  colour - bigger and brighter for bigger effects. Runes on your pickaxe and clothes never chatter: runes that keep
  things going (Haste, Mending, Radiance, Magnet and the like) only glint, near a player one sign shows at most every two seconds, and a rune on your
  own body draws a smaller, quieter sign. The client option "Rune signs" can hide your own signs or all of them.
- Runes show what they do, quietly: a Drill pickaxe's lightning strikes every block it takes, Smelting sets the blocks
  alight for a moment, Erosion, Passage and Quake crumble the stone away block by block, Forge and Gemstone lift hot
  sparks and gem light out of the rock, Cache sends the drops into your pocket as pieces of themselves, Insight sends a
  wave through the stone. On the other tools: a felled tree comes down log by log, saplings and seeds rise green,
  Chain Lightning and Flash strike as bolts, Withering draws the foe's life to you, Spoils glints gold. Each has its
  own soft sound, played once even when many blocks go at once.
- The same goes for the rest: clothes heal, cleanse and ward off fire in rising light, a Mirror robe strikes back as a
  bolt, boots cushion a fall with a gust; the grapple's Gravity drags its catch in along a stream of light; machines'
  runes rise hot, golden, green or as steam where they work; the Null Lantern and the Void Anchors bite with thin bolts.
- Learning a rune from a scroll or from research notes: the rune writes itself in the air before your eyes inside a
  hexagon, holds for a moment and flies into you.
- The altar's circle: when a ritual starts, a hexagonal sigil lights up on the ground, sweeping round from the north and
  drawing a line to every pedestal, stele and jar of the circle. Light runs along the lines into the altar - brightest
  from the pedestal or jar being drawn - and the sigil turns faster as the work goes on. A stalled ritual reddens it, a
  collapse cracks it apart, a finished one throws it outwards as it fades.
- The altar draws essence from its jars as living streams, like every other machine that draws from jars.
- A pedestal item drawn into the altar comes apart into pieces of itself: each piece tears out upwards, swings over in
  its own arc and whirls into the altar's item, leaving a few sparks behind. The item no longer crumbles as if it broke.
- A working circle breathes: sparks rise slowly off the lines of the sigil and motes of light gather into the work.
- Essence on the move can be seen: a pulse of light runs through brass pipes in the colour of what they carry (water
  too); pouring a bottle into a jar or filling it from one, and every device that pays from the jars around it (Binding
  Chalk, Rift Gates, the World Anchor, winding a Recall Charm), shows a living stream from the jar.
- The alembic: steam climbs out of the boiling flask into the head, the distillate drips from the beak in the colour of
  what it holds, and an item boiling down lets its essence rise off the water.
- The separator's drop through the stopcock falls where you can see it, not only hear it.

## [1.4.0] - 2026-10-28
Ready for modpacks: every recipe can be changed by data packs and recipe scripts, shared materials go by common tags,
machines respect claims, and other mods' pipes, quests and slots work with HexTale.

### Added
- The mod's recipes are ordinary recipe types now: `hextale:hex_crafting` (Hex Workbench), `hextale:infusion` (the
  altar), `hextale:alloying` (Hex Furnace), `hextale:crucible`, `hextale:brewing`, `hextale:lab_experiment` and the
  new `hextale:awakening` (which block hextech dust wakes into what). Data packs, [KubeJS](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/kubejs)
  and [KubeGroovy](https://mods.uncandango.dev/kubegroovy/) can change, add and remove them. The diary still finds a
  recipe a pack replaced under another name.
- Common tags: brass, hexsteel, hextech dust and crystal, stripped magic wood; recipes ask for brass, zinc, hexsteel,
  iron, copper, glass and other shared materials by tag, so the same metals from other mods work.
- Item tags for the Research Table's parchment (`hextale:research_paper`) and the Separator's fuel
  (`hextale:separator_fuels`); biome tags for sphalerite, struck geodes and bloom caves
  (`hextale:has_sphalerite`, `has_struck_geodes`, `has_bloom_caves`) - empty one to turn that feature off.
- Claims are respected on servers: tools, gadgets, machines and the Void ask claim mods (FTB Chunks, Open Parties and
  Claims and any mod using NeoForge's block events) before they break or place blocks. Devices act in the name of the
  player who placed them; the Void and lightning act as "[HexTale]", so server owners can allow or deny them.
- Server config: `blightChangesBlocks`, `voidMitesSpawn`, `voidlingsSpawn` and `breaches`, all on by default; block tag
  `hextale:void_immune` for blocks the blight, the healing land and the Hexcore's lightning never touch.
- Pipes, tanks and hoppers from other mods: the Crucible and Alembic take water, the Crucible takes items as if they
  were dropped in, the Crystal Battery and the Hex Workbench's socket take crystals, the Hex Lapidary takes crystals and
  discs and gives out finished stones. Modded buckets and tanks work on the Crucible and Alembic. The Pump fills a tank
  from another mod on top of it; the Experience Condenser offers its experience as liquid experience when a mod adds one.
- Pure essence leaves and enters the mod as a fluid, so tanks, pipes and storage networks of other mods can hold and
  move it: an Essence Jar (also the Big one) gives essence fluid only while it holds a single essence - mixtures stay
  in the jar and are never split - and takes the essence fluid its sticker allows, mixing it in like a pipe. The
  Creative Essence Jar gives every essence without end. Essence fluids are named after their essence and tinted in its
  colour; JEI lists one per essence.
- The Sorting Matrix knows common modded chests, barrels and drawers.
- For quests: advancement triggers `hextale:research_completed`, `guide_step`, `guide_event` and `ritual_finished`,
  and operator commands `/hextale research grant|revoke` and `/hextale guide grant|reset`.
- JEI: a result's tooltip names the research that opens it; recipes move into the Hex Workbench's grid with the
  transfer button; the workbench and the Hex Furnace show up for vanilla crafting and smelting. Jade shows the
  workbench's charge.
- [Curios](https://www.curseforge.com/minecraft/mc-mods/curios) (optional): the Researcher's Goggles work from a head
  slot and show on the head there, over a helmet too.
- A guide for modpack makers: [docs/modpack.md](https://github.com/Pocky-l/HexTale/blob/main/docs/modpack.md). Released item and block ids stay: a renamed one
  keeps working under its old name.

### Changed
- The Spawner Cage, multiblock forming and dust awakening tell you when a place is protected.

### Fixed
- A Rift Gate no longer takes fluids while it is not linked, and pipes can no longer pull fluid out of it.
- The Hex Lapidary starts a fresh cut when a hopper puts a crystal in.

## [1.3.0] - 2026-10-28
Glassy scars and the first gadgets: the way into hextech no longer waits for a storm, the hextech tools come right
after the first researches, every device takes runes that change what it gives, and the Thinning grows from working
essence.

### Added
- Struck geodes: rare places where lightning hit an amethyst geode long ago, a few blocks under the ground - about
  one in every 300 blocks, never two close together. From afar
  they give themselves away: a shallow crater fused into fulgurite, glassy fulgurite spikes standing on its rim and
  woken hextech crystals glowing cold blue on its floor, throwing sparks and crackling. A pit in the middle of the
  crater drops down a fulgurite channel into the hollow, where the amethyst around the point where the bolt broke in
  has turned into Dormant Hextech and hextech crystals.
- Hextech crystals in the four amethyst sizes (small, medium and large buds and the cluster): amethyst the strike
  woke all the way. They glow and spark; the cluster breaks into an unstable orb (Fortune gives more), buds need Silk
  Touch.
- Fulgurite Spike: a knobbly, branching glassy tube of fused earth; spikes stack into taller ones (base, middle, tip).
- Dormant Hextech: cold blue amethyst that keeps a faint charge - sparks crawl over it now and then with a quiet
  crystal hum. A pickaxe breaks it into unstable hextech orbs (Fortune gives more, Silk Touch keeps the block), and a
  lightning rod's strike wakes it like amethyst.
- Fulgurite: earth and sand fused into rough glass. Lightning that goes into sand fuses a short column of it, so it
  can be found after any storm too. Smelted, it clears into Fulgurite Glass (smoky amber glass with the bolt's thread
  inside) and its panes.
- Scorched Calcite, the struck geode's shell, with a building set: polished, bricks with stairs, slab and wall, and a
  chiseled block with a carved bolt. Calcite smelted in a furnace scorches too.
- A diary step "Glassy Scars" next to the lightning rod's: a second road to the first hextech.
- Hextech Tuning Fork: two brass prongs over a raw orb, made at the Hex Workbench. Held up (hold right click), it
  hums higher and louder the nearer the hextech in the ground, and a thread of light points the way; struck against a
  block (Shift + right click) it listens for that block instead. It takes runes at the altar: Shift (hears farther),
  Insight (what it hears glows through the stone), Signal (hears several at once), Gemstone (untuned, hears ores
  too).
- Dust Charge: three pinches of hextech dust twisted into paper and tied (three at the Hex Workbench). Thrown or shot
  from a dispenser, it sticks where it lands, hisses for a second and a half and bursts in a cold flash: rock, ores,
  gravel and dirt around it crack apart and drop as if mined with an iron pickaxe, while builds, wood and obsidian stay
  whole. Creatures close by take a small sting and a push.
- Levitation Plate: a raw orb set in a brass ring on a stone plate (Hex Workbench). The air above it lifts whatever
  stands there - players, animals, dropped items - up to six blocks or the first block above, and holds it there,
  gently bobbing; sneaking sinks you back down, and nobody in the column is hurt by falling. Its brass ring floats
  and turns over the dais; while it carries someone it spins up, glows and sends rings of light climbing the column
  to the height it lifts to, motes stream up and fade where the column ends, the air swells as you are taken up, the
  column hums while it holds you and sighs as it lets you go. Runes: Shift (a taller column), Motor (a faster lift), Breath (whoever steps
  out floats down slowly for a while), Cache (what floats at the top goes into the chest or barrel that caps the
  column) and Slumber (creatures in the column hang asleep and wake slowly after).
- Ward Glass and Veil Glass (four at the Hex Workbench): glass with a rune of barrier that chooses who passes. Ward
  Glass stops players but lets creatures and dropped items through; Veil Glass lets players walk through and stops
  creatures. Creatures plan their way through Ward Glass and around Veil Glass.
- Floating Stone (two at the Hex Workbench from smooth stone, dust and a feather): set against a block like any stone,
  or into thin air - with nothing aimed at, it settles three blocks ahead and stays there.
- Null Lantern (nullstone round a raw orb, after the Hextech Purifier): its cold violet light drinks the magic that lets
  hostile creatures form - none spawn by themselves within 24 blocks (spawners, eggs and those who walk in still come).
  It stands or hangs like a lantern. Runes: Shift (a wider quiet, up to 64), Hush (no phantoms over sleepless players
  in it), Banishing (hostile creatures inside take a small bite now and then), Silence (sounds inside go soft, down
  to nothing; strong enough, no vibration inside reaches sculk sensors, shriekers or wardens).
- Impulse Plate (two at the Hex Workbench): whatever steps on it is thrown up and along its brass arrow, placed facing
  the way you look - or, aimed like a faith plate, onto a spot of your choosing: right click it with an empty hand,
  then right click the block it should throw to (the arc to the block under your crosshair shows while you choose,
  red when out of reach). An aimed plate works out the throw for whatever stands on it - player, sheep or dropped
  stone - so it lands on the target, and the landing is soft. Its launch panel snaps up with a deep thump and a
  whoosh, the arc flashes along the way, and the target is marked by a ring of light whenever you hold a plate or
  wear the researcher's goggles. Runes: Shift (higher, and farther targets: 24 blocks, up to 64), Motor (farther,
  and a quicker flight), Breath (a soft landing for unaimed throws too), Haste (the thrown keeps
  running fast for a while) and Banishing (a hostile creature that steps on is flung back and high, with no soft
  landing).
- Recall Charm (a clock at the Hextech Altar; replaces the "Recall Device" idea): used, it puts you back where you
  stood five seconds ago in the same world, then rests for half a minute. Each return spends one of its six windings;
  right click a jar of rift essence with it to wind it again (4 mB a winding). Runes: Shift (up to 24 seconds back),
  Haste (a shorter rest), Mending (heals on the way back), Fireproofing (puts out the flames and keeps fire off for a
  while) and Dam (now and then a return costs no winding).
- Soul Flask (at the Hextech Altar): right click a creature to fold it into the glass, whole - name, saddle, everything;
  right click a block to let it out there. No players and no bosses. Runes: Healing (it comes out healed), Growth (a
  young one comes out grown), Slumber (a hostile one comes out dazed).
- Spawner Cage (at the Hextech Altar): pressed to a monster spawner it folds the spawner in, whole - what it spawns
  and how; pressed to a block it sets the spawner there, and the cage burns out doing it.
- Lesser Rift Gates (a pair from the Hextech Altar; replaces the "Shadow Coffer" idea): upright rings of nullstone and
  brass. Press a hextech crystal to one gate and it becomes a Rift Link Crystal that remembers it; press that to a
  second gate and the two are tied by a link of their own, wherever they stand - even in another dimension. Whatever
  then passes into one ring comes out of the other's front: players, creatures, thrown items, and whatever hoppers or
  pylons feed into it (items drop into a chest or hopper under the far gate if there is one). Liquids go through
  too: essence piped or poured into one gate flows out into the pipes and jars around the other, and fluids from
  other mods' pipes and tanks come out into the tanks beside the far gate. A linked ring glows
  with rift light; break one gate and the other's empty ring crackles, alone, until it is linked anew. Right click a
  gate with an empty hand to ask where it leads. Every crossing is paid in rift essence from the jars within five
  blocks of the gate it leaves through: 1 mB for a player or a creature, 1 mB for every eight items, a little for
  essence and fluids. A gate whose jars are dry lets nothing through: its ring only smokes and spits thrown things
  back out.
- World Anchor (at the Hextech Altar): keeps its chunk loaded and ticking while it stands, even with nobody near.
  It eats earth essence from the jars around it every minute - half a mB for every chunk it holds, at least 1 - and
  when they run dry it falls asleep and lets its chunks go until it is fed again. Right click it with an empty hand
  to see what it holds and eats. Runes: Shift (3x3, then 5x5 chunks), Haste (time runs faster there), Fertility
  (plants there grow now and then) and Dam (now and then a minute costs no essence).
- Disintegrator (at the Hex Workbench): a deepslate bowl that unmakes whatever is dropped, thrown or piped into it
  into unknown essence and pours it into the jar or pipe below - fuel for the Chem Burner. The unmaking thins the land
  around. Runes: Motor (faster), Dam (more essence), Radiance (experience now and then), Hush (less leaks into the
  land).
- Magnet, a new rune for the Researcher's Trousers: loose items and experience around you drift to you (4 to 10
  blocks by its stars; crouch to let them lie, what you just dropped stays).
- Sponge, a new rune for the Greedy Funnel: now and then a swallowed death leaves its essence too - what its drops
  are made of soaks into a jar beside the funnel, the drops themselves still go to the chest.
- Grapple Gauntlet (at the Hex Workbench): hold right click to throw its hextech claw - it bites into the block you
  look at and holds you on a real rope. The rope only pulls when stretched, so you drop, swing through the bottom and
  up the other side; release to let go with all your speed and fling yourself on, then throw again mid-air to chain
  swings. Jump reels the rope in, sneak lets it out. The cord sags when slack and pulls tight and bright under your
  weight, and everyone around sees and hears the claw fly, bite, reel and spring back. Runes: Shift (a longer rope),
  Motor (reels in faster), Breath (no fall damage for a while after letting go), Gravity (the claw bites a creature too and yanks it
  to you) and Blades (the claw cuts what it bites).
- The gadgets are worked out at the Research Table before they can be made: the Levitation and Impulse Plates, Ward

### Added

- The world takes a good deal before anything shows; past that, haze: a lilac veil low over the ground, dark motes

  (never the last one). A potion of
  healing, drunk, splashed or lingering, makes them all let go. You can see them on you.

  the edges of sight darkening, and the land murmuring from somewhere around.

  alive it is. Dry twigs on clean land; it stirs long before the land shows anything, leafs out, blooms around a hollow

  comparator beside it reads how thin the world is there. It has its own step in the researcher's guide.

  machines draw from it like from any jar. Breaking any of its blocks
  takes it apart into its glass and slabs; the essence inside is lost and no jar drops. The ground under it stays lit. The guide shows the structure layer by layer.
- Brass Pipes - slim tubes with small flanges - carry essence and water and join a machine only where it has a port;
  the machine's model grows a matching joint there. Sneak-use a pipe's side with an empty hand to set it: next to
  another pipe it shuts the joint (both pipes show capped stubs) or opens it again; next to a machine or jar it cycles
  both ways -> in only (green collar) -> out only (blue collar; a jar on that side is pumped out into the pipes) -> shut.
  The goggles and the pipe's tooltip tell what the side you look at does. The Drain Valve pours whatever reaches it out into the world while
  its handwheel is open; essence it pours onto stone soaks in and turns it into magic stone.
- Researcher's Goggles: worn on the head, they show the essences inside a jar, pipe or machine you look at; looking at a
  Research Table shows its ink while coloured streams run from each jar to the orb.
- Liquid essence swirls with an animated texture, glows at its surface, darkens in the depths and bubbles. The
  alembic's flask and the separator's vessel are round glass with framed panes, like the jar.

**Rune works**
- Hextech Altar circle: the altar, Pedestals within six blocks, Rune Steles within seven and Essence Jars within eight.
  With an item on the altar, a stele lists only the runes that do something for it (the runes a pickaxe or a
  pylon takes, and those its rituals ask for) in a tidy, scrollable list: each with its name, your stars, what it does
  on that item, its number and the essence it draws at your stars (a tooltip shows all three star levels with their essence, what it cannot share an item with
  and, for a ritual, the essence the whole ritual draws). The circle stays sensible: a rune stands on one stele only,
  an item never gets more runes than its slots (the header counts them, e.g. 2/3 on a pylon; raising the stars
  of a rune it already has takes no slot) and never two incompatible runes - such runes are dimmed with the reason; an empty altar shows the whole codex.
  Sneak-use the altar with an empty hand (or give it a redstone pulse) to start a ritual: the rings turn around the
  centre item, the pedestal items are drawn in one by one, then the essences one after another, while shards of each
  stele's hieroglyph peel off and wind in a corkscrew into the work, white-hot, cooling into the rune's colour, and
  the centre becomes something new. The runes go into the work: after a ritual every stele of the circle goes dark
  and waits for a rune to be chosen again. A ritual short of essence stalls and, left too long, collapses. Ghosts of the items a ritual
  still lacks hover over the free pedestals.
- Block of Brass.
- Hextech pickaxes: the Hextech Pickaxe (iron strength), the Refined Hextech Pickaxe (diamond) and the Radiant Hextech
  Pickaxe (netherite, fireproof), each made at the altar from the one before - runes and enchantments carry over. Their
  crystal glows in the colour of the runes they carry.
- Runes on a pickaxe, like enchantments: put the pickaxe alone on the altar, choose runes on rune steles and start the
  ritual - each rune is burned in, paid only with its own essence (more for more stars and for harder runes). Up to
  four runes on any pickaxe; thirteen runes of the codex take to a pickaxe, and a rune is as strong as its stars. A
  stele with more stars raises a rune the pickaxe already has. Some runes cannot share a pickaxe, and only one of them
  may answer the right click. A grindstone takes the runes off again.
- The guide shows a turning 3D altar burning the chosen rune into a pickaxe or the smelter; pick any rune they take to
  see what it does, how strong it is at one, two and three stars, what it costs and which runes it is incompatible
  with. JEI shows the same for every rune, next to every pickaxe recipe.
- Magic stone: stone that has drunk magic - flat plates of a dusky violet stone with nothing set into it. Throw two lapis
  lazuli into a boiling crucible, then stone, cobblestone, deepslate, tuff, andesite, diorite, granite, calcite or
  smooth stone: each block sinks, takes 6 mB of the magic, and rises out as magic stone in a burst of violet light. An open
  Drain Valve over such stone soaks it too: the essence it pours soaks in where the stream lands, one block per 16 mB. Polished magic stone, magic bricks with stairs, slab and wall, and chiseled magic bricks with the
  circle's hexagon cut into them: the stone of the ritual circle.

**Item transport: hex pylons**
- Instead of a clumsy mechanical arm, the pull of hextech: four pylons made at the altar, each its own shape - a low
  brass bowl that catches, a flat plate with claws that throws, a calcite and copper column with an amethyst crystal
  that passes on, a glass bulb that pours essence.
- Gathering Pylon: set on a chest, a barrel, a furnace or any container, it reaches for the items lying around (6
  blocks, more with runes) and lowers them into what it stands on - a furnace gets ore through its top. With nothing
  under it, it sends them along its links.
- Sending Pylon: stands on a storage and sends what it gives out (a furnace's result, a chest's anything) to the places
  you link. Right click the pylon, then right click a storage: a chest or a barrel simply fills, a furnace (or a blast
  furnace, a smoker) sorts what comes - what it can cook into the input, fuel into the fuel slot; a gathering pylon on a
  furnace does the same. Up to four links, tried in order; the same clicks again unlink one, sneak + right click with an
  empty hand unlinks all. Clicking the air or a block that cannot take items stops linking and does what it always does;
  with a block in hand a right click on a pylon places the block. A broken block drops its links by itself, and sneak +
  right click with an empty hand on a linked block unties it from every pylon. The item tooltip tells how.
- Pylons belong to whoever placed them and deliver on their behalf: they feed the Alembic, the Separator, the Hex
  Furnace, the Research Table and Pedestals like a hopper would, and the Alembic boils what they bring into its own
  essences only if the pylon's owner (online) has studied it. The goggles show a pylon's owner.
- A pylon with several links takes them in turn: each move goes to the next link that takes something, so every
  linked chest, machine or jar gets its share.
- A Sending Pylon waits until its target has room for a whole batch (a furnace with 62 ore gets nothing until 8 fit),
  and what is already flying to a target counts against its room, so no extra items fly there only to come back.
- Relay Pylon: neither takes nor gives - it catches items and essence sent to it and passes them on to its own links,
  up to eight relays in a chain. Made of calcite, copper ingots, amethyst and a lightning rod.
- Siphon Pylon: set on an Essence Jar, it draws the essence up into its glass bulb (swirling in the jar's colour) and
  sends it as glowing motes arcing along its links, like the essence drawn into the altar - into other jars, a separator, anything that takes
  essence from pipes; relays pass the stream on.
- Filter, made at the Hextech Altar (a brass ingot with string, brass nuggets and an amethyst shard): right click it to set it up - nine slots (click with an item to put it in), "only these" or "all but
  these", matched by kind or exactly (enchantments, contents), and numbers: keep N of each item in the source, or fill
  a target only up to N (scroll over a slot to set N). Right click a pylon with it to set it in (the one there comes
  back), sneak + right click to take it out. On a gathering pylon it picks what to gather, on a sending pylon what to
  send, on a relay what may pass - a few relays with filters sort a storeroom. The filter's items circle the pylon's heart.
- Essence Sticker, made at the Hextech Altar (paper, slime and an amethyst shard, six at a time): right click to pick its essence from all of them. On an Essence Jar the jar takes only that essence
  (from pipes, bottles and siphons alike); sneak + right click with an empty hand peels it off. On a siphon or a relay
  it lets only that essence through, pure. Pylons never split a mixture - it always travels whole; separating essences
  stays the separator's work.
- Sorting Matrix, made at the Hextech Altar (a hextech crystal, an observer, a comparator, glass and brass): a lattice
  of eight lenses turning on its corner around a hextech gem inside a brass frame. Set it on a chest - the dump chest -
  and it reads what lies there and sends every item home, up to eight kinds at a time: to any chest, barrel or shulker
  box within 6 blocks that already holds that item (want a chest for cobblestone? put one cobblestone in it), or along
  its links - through relays too, and a relay whose Filter names an item makes the chest behind it that item's home.
  Linked furnaces get the ores and food nobody keeps, and more of the fuel they already burn. What has no home stays
  in the dump chest; a chest that carries a pylon is never filled. Linking another pylon to the matrix sends into its
  chest, so gathering and sending pylons feed it. It holds eight links; Shift widens its reach, Motor quickens it,
  Lift moves more at once. With the researcher's goggles, the storages it sorts into show a turning ring on their lids.
- Pylon runes, burned in at the altar like a smelter's: Shift (reach and link range), Motor (pace), Lift (items per
  move), Lens. A broken pylon keeps its runes.
- The magic fetches every item: a tendril of light, two strands twisting around a swaying line, reaches out of the gem,
  closes a turning sigil around the item, draws back and drags it in, swinging a little; the gem flares as it drinks the
  item in, and on to the next gem or container another tendril stretches out and pulls it along. Several items get
  several tendrils, one after another. Links show as gold threads while you sneak or hold a pylon; while linking, a
  thread runs to your crosshair. Soft whooshes for pulling and sending, a low touch for landing.
- Researcher's Goggles: every link around shows while worn; looking at a pylon shows its reach, pace, link range, its
  links (where to, how far) and runes, a gathering pylon's reach lights up as a ring on the ground; looking at a linked
  block shows which pylons send to it.
- Decorative: Magic Stone Pillar (like a quartz pillar) and Brass Grate (see-through, like a copper grate).
- Built to be cheap: items move straight between inventories in one transaction (only the fetching is drawn), idle
  pylons look four times rarer, and the clients get one small message per move.

**Farming: the Whirl Reaper**
- Whirl Reaper: a brass mast on a foot of whorled magic stone, three framed sails turning round it and a hextech
  crystal floating on top in a little whirl of its own. Researched at the Research Table and made at the altar
  (hextech crystal, feathers, an iron hoe, brass, polished magic stone; wind, plant and crystal essence). Set among
  the beds, it sends a whirl of wind to the nearest ripe plant within 4 blocks (3 up and down): the whirl leaves the
  crystal, flies over to the plant and stands on it as a turning funnel of light, crumbs of the plant swirling up it.
  - Wheat, carrots, potatoes, beetroot (and modded crops), nether wart, cocoa and sweet berries are reaped where
    they stand: they start again from a sprout, one seed stays in the ground, the harvest falls on their own block.
  - A melon or pumpkin on its stem is lifted off into the whirl, spun and broken open where it lay.
  - Sugar cane, cactus and bamboo three blocks tall are twisted off above the root; the pieces rise turning round
    the funnel and drop beside the root (a cactus would burn them on top of itself).
  - Every whirl drinks wind essence from a jar within 5 blocks (1 mB for four whirls; feathers boil into wind); the
    jar's stream runs into the crystal. Without wind it stands becalmed, its sails barely turning.
  - A Gathering Pylon nearby picks up the harvest. Runes at the altar: Shift (wider field), Motor (whirls more often),
    Lens; a broken reaper keeps them. Right click shows its field, pace and wind; Jade too.
  - Light on the server: it reads its field block by block only when it looks, and an idle reaper looks more and
    more rarely; the clients play each whirl from one small message.
- Whorled Magic Stone: polished magic stone with a carved spiral (stonecutter).
- Hex Sawmill: a dark oak stump with a hextech saw disc of blued steel turning in a slot under a brass guard, a
  crystal floating over it. Researched at the Research Table, made at the altar (hextech crystal, iron axe, dark oak
  logs, an oak sapling, brass; wind, wood and metal essence). It throws a glowing copy of its disc at the nearest grown
  tree within 6 blocks (3 up and down): the disc flies over, saws through the trunk in sawdust and sparks, and the whole
  tree tips over away from the mill, slowly and then faster, and breaks apart where it hits the ground. Every log and
  every leaf of it is taken; the drops (logs, saplings, sticks, apples) land on its stump and a sapling from them is
  planted where it stood - four on the four feet of a big tree when four dropped.
  - Only trees grown from the ground are felled: logs on dirt with grown leaves. Log walls, pillars and trees whose
    leaves were placed by hand are never touched; roots under the ground stay.
  - Each tree takes 2 mB of wind essence from a jar within 5 blocks (the jar's stream runs into the crystal); without
    wind it stands becalmed. Runes at the altar: Shift (farther trees), Motor (saws more often), Lens; a broken sawmill
    keeps them. Right click shows its grove, pace and wind; Jade too. Sounds: the disc whirring off, the bite into
    wood, the trunk creaking over, the crash and the rustle of leaves.
